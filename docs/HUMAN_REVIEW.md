# Human review and seller reputation

Members can request help with a resolution or captured-payment refund from the Human review panel on their Pact. This opens a durable case, records its shared reason, notifies both parties, and suspends competing mutual financial proposals. One open case per Pact and request-content idempotency prevent duplicate cases. An interrupted response is an unknown outcome: refresh to inspect the saved case before retrying. Chat retries preserve their request identifier.

The developer desk is /workspace/support. Configure PACT_REVIEWER_SUBJECTS with verified Clerk subjects, never client-supplied roles. A reviewer uses their normal Pact account and needs no personal PayPal account. An assigned reviewer cannot belong to the Pact or moderate a review involving themselves. The selected local reviewer account is configured privately in .env; no personal identity is committed here.

Buyer and seller conversations are separate. Parties see only messages to their own conversation; assigned reviewers see both. The case reason and final decision are shared. Existing Evidence uploads remain shared Pact records; referencing a file in a private message does not make that file private from the other Pact member. Staff can inspect the original processed, clean files through scoped, expiring, audited downloads. Quarantined or foreign-Pact files are denied.

A reviewer must contact both parties and receive both responses before deciding. The response window lasts 72 hours from assignment. After it expires, a missing response requires an explicit recorded explanation. Decisions require the current case version and include the current evidence snapshot, server-computed integer amount, immutable reason, reviewer identity and exact proposal hash/version. Reassignments and appeals require additional workflow; an existing decision cannot be silently edited.

A human decision may close the review without refund, refund remaining captured funds, or refund a percentage. The seller has no veto over an authorized staff decision. Existing voluntary, two-party resolution remains a separate path. AI cannot authorize either path. A party cannot retire or cancel a queued reviewer-authorized refund by changing evidence or making another proposal. The worker independently validates staff authority, case/resolution linkage, snapshot, capture and available funds. Revoked staff permission fails closed. Queued/submitted decisions are not completed refunds. Official PayPal confirmation and shared receipts establish completion; ambiguous attempts require readback, never a second POST.

This implementation is Sandbox only and refunds the capture belonging to the configured developer merchant. It cannot reverse money paid to an unrelated merchant or route money to other Pact members. Human staffing and response availability are operational responsibilities; the UI does not promise a person or guaranteed refund when reviewers are unconfigured.

Seller reviews are one immutable review per settled, confirmed Pact, submitted by its payer for the other party. Ratings are 1-5 with bounded text; self reviews, unrelated users and unsettled payments are rejected. Directory/profile pages are visible to signed-in Pact users and expose seller display names, review counts and review text; no Pact titles, private evidence, contact details or provider identifiers are exposed. Reviewers can hide abusive reviews with an immutable moderation reason, and cannot moderate their own reviews. Review authors must confirm publication and avoid private details. Sandbox transaction reviews are clearly labeled; ratings are experiences, not guarantees.

Validation uses isolated PostgreSQL and Redis via scripts/test-integration-local.mjs, plus UI privacy/idempotency tests. Provider doubles are confined to tests. No real refund is needed or authorized by merely testing this feature.

Reviewer availability is checked per Pact, excluding its own parties. A configured staff login alone does not imply an independent reviewer exists for every agreement. The help panel explicitly explains when no eligible reviewer is configured, and preserves saved requests without promising a response.
